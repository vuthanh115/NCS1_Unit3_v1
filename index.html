❓ Big Question

"What is your favorite way to celebrate?"

(Bạn thích tổ chức ăn mừng như thế nào?)

☀️ Warm Up

  • People follow traditions. (Mọi người làm theo truyền thống.)
  • A Thanksgiving parade is a tradition. (Diễu hành lễ Tạ ơn là một truyền thống.)

Key Ideas:

  • Families have picnics.
  • People give cards to each other.
  • Tell the class about a tradition in your family.

🎧 Nghe Words to Know:

📖 Celebration Time!

I am having lots and lots of fun! It is celebration time!

It is the New Year. It can be named for an ox, rat, or ram. Around the world, it is celebration time!

I can have a fun time at a carnival. I can put on a big blue wig and a mask to hide.

I can smile and dress up. I can dress up in a red and green costume. It is fun to dress up in a hat.

On July 4, I can dress up in red, white, and blue. I can wave a flag.

Mom, Dad, and I are gazing at the sky. Pop! Pop! Pop! Pop! Pop! Pop!

In Ghana, tribes plant crops. It is celebration time. Lots of drums go tap, tap, tap!

🤔 Think It Over

1. What can the New Year be named for?
2. Why do people like to dress up for celebrations?

💡 Strategy: Summarize (Tóm tắt)
Tell the most important information in a story.

📝 Words to Know

1. First they got red envelopes.
2. Then they took a ride with friends.
3. They saw the dragons dancing.
4. The parade was so much fun!

📖 Chinese New Year

Every year people say, “Hooray!” Because the Chinese New Year lasts for 15 days.

First, it’s time for the family to eat. Sweet rice cakes are a great treat!

Next, Chen gives Kai a sweet snack, and then Kai gives him one back.

Then Grandma gives them envelopes that are red. “There are coins inside!” they said.

Look at the cute dragon the children made. They bring it with them to the parade.

At the parade they see dragons and lions dancing.

On the fifteenth day, they look up in the night sky to see lots of lights passing by.

Finally, the Chinese New Year is done. Everyone is tired but had lots of fun.

🤔 Think It Over

1. How long does the Chinese New Year last?
2. What does Grandma give the kids?
3. What do they see at the parade?
💡 Strategy: Visualizing (Hình dung)
Pictures you see in your head while you read.

📝 Words to Know

1. A country's flag is a symbol.
2. People live and work in their community.
3. The American flag is red, white, and blue.
4. What does our flag look like?

📖 A Flag

A flag is a symbol of a country. It shows that we belong to a community.

I see a flag at our school. It has a black stripe, a red stripe, and a yellow stripe.

I see a flag at the soccer game. It is green, yellow, and blue. It has 27 white stars.

I see a flag at the zoo. It is red, white, and blue.

There is even an Olympic flag! It has 5 colored rings.

I see a blue ring, a yellow ring, a black ring, a green ring, and a red ring.

Flags are seen in lots of places. Look around. Do you see your country’s flag?

🤔 Think It Over

1. What does a flag symbolize?
2. What does the flag at the soccer game look like?
3. What colors are the rings on the Olympic flag?
